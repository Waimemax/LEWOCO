About Chakula Chap

Chakula Chap is your local fast food restaurant

Welcome to Chakula Chap---one of the jolliest fast food restaurants on earth. Our menu offers many of your favorite comfort foods including burgers, fried chicken, french fries and more. We're a fast-food brand built for people who love good food and hate waiting. We serve fresh, tasty meals made fast—bacause great food should fit your lifestyle.
